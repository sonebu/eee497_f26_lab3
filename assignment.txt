EEE 497 - Lab 03
Read a Morse tone under the noise

A pilot tone is on the air the whole time. Your tone is at a fixed offset
above it. That number will be given to you. Your tone is keyed in Morse 
and it is weaker than the noise. The transmission is looping. 

Your job is to report the decoded text from a recording.

It's recommended to record about 4 seconds. Tune to 433 MHz.

Ubuntu:

    rtl_sdr -f 433000000 -s 2048000 -g 49 -n 8192000 capture.iq

Windows, from the folder that contains rtl_sdr.exe:

    rtl_sdr.exe -f 433000000 -s 2048000 -g 49 -n 8192000 capture.iq

you have the following tools for this lab to help you:

    spectrum(x, fs)              frequency and power of one long FFT
    pilot_hz(x, fs)              frequency of the loudest line, 20 to 120 kHz
    cut_into_units(x, fs, start) one row per 10 ms, from sample start
    runs(on)                     stretches of equal values, and their lengths
    decode(on)                   text from one True or False per unit
    plot_line, plot_histogram    save a plot to a file

Morse timing, in units, is given below --> One unit is 10 ms:

    a dot is 1 unit
    a dash is 3 units
    the gap inside a letter is 1 unit
    the gap between letters is 3 units
    the gap between words is 7 units

Nothing is sent from 20 to 40 kHz below the pilot. Thus, your detector, 
run there, will give you the noise stats. 

The deliverable is your complete code package, showing all intermediate steps
towards decoding the morse code you receive, as either plots or terminal prints.
